VaultCell is a password manager built on a zero-knowledge architecture. That is not a marketing phrase here — it determines what data exists on our side at all, and therefore what this policy can even promise. Read the short version first: your vault contents are encrypted on your device, we only ever receive ciphertext, and we never receive your master password.

2. What we collect

2.1 Vault content

Everything you store in VaultCell — passwords, passkeys, cards, secure notes, and any tags or folder names — is encrypted on your device before it is written to disk or transmitted. We operate no server that holds your vault; synchronization runs through Apple's CloudKit, into the private database tied to your own Apple ID.

Your master password is never transmitted, never uploaded, and never stored by us. It exists only in your memory and, transiently, in your device's memory while the vault is unlocked. We cannot read your vault, and we cannot help you recover it.

2.2 iCloud and CloudKit

Synchronization uses Apple's CloudKit framework. Your encrypted vault is written to your personal iCloud private database. We have no administrative access to that database, no export tooling against it, and no ability to enumerate its contents.

Where you share a vault with another person, sharing is handled by CloudKit's sharing mechanism. Invitees receive the same ciphertext you hold; they can only decrypt it with the key material you share with them separately.

Storage region. For users whose Apple ID country or region is mainland China, iCloud and CloudKit services are operated by Guizhou-Cloud Big Data Industrial Development Co., Ltd. (云上贵州), and vault data remains stored within mainland China. For all other users, data may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries where Apple operates its infrastructure.

2.3 Diagnostics, crash reports, and device information

We receive a limited set of aggregate technical data through Apple so we can find and fix the bugs that matter. This data never includes vault contents, master passwords, or keys, and it is not linked to you as an individual.

Diagnostics reach us only through Apple, never through our own collection. VaultCell ships no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and we operate no telemetry endpoint of our own. That switch is yours alone. While it is off we see nothing about your device — no crash reports, no session counts, nothing. We do not prompt you to turn it on, we cannot change it on your behalf, and enabling it is never a condition of using anything in VaultCell — every feature works identically whether it is on or off. It is not linked to your identity and we cannot single out any individual device.

We do not use MetricKit. MetricKit would let us receive diagnostic payloads inside the app and send them to our own server, and unlike Apple's reporting it is not switched off by any setting you control. We have chosen not to use it for that reason.

Website icons are not diagnostics and are not covered by this section: those requests go from your device straight to the website you saved, not to us. See section 2.8.

2.8 Website icons

VaultCell shows a small icon beside each item so you can find things quickly. Where that icon comes from matters, so here is exactly what happens.

  • Built in, and offline. Icons for the most common websites and apps ship inside VaultCell. For those items the icon is drawn from the app itself, and no network request of any kind is made.
  • A generated fallback, shown immediately. If we do not ship an icon for a site, VaultCell first draws one locally — a letter tile in a colour derived from the domain — so an item is never left blank while anything else is pending.
  • Automatic fetching. Outside mainland China, by default VaultCell then contacts the domain saved on that item and downloads the icon that site publishes — for example https://www.paypal.com/favicon.ico. The request goes directly from your device to that website. It does not pass through us, or through any server or cache we operate. We cannot see it, we do not log it, and we hold no record of which items you have. Where your App Store country is mainland China, automatic fetching is off by default, and VaultCell shows only the built-in and generated icons unless you switch it on yourself.

What that means for you. We receive nothing. But the requests are not invisible. Each website you have saved sees the IP address a request came from, and anyone able to observe your network traffic — your internet provider, your DNS resolver, a network operator, or whoever runs the Wi-Fi or cellular network you are on — can see the domains being requested. HTTPS encrypts what is transferred, not who you are talking to. That is why you can turn it off: with icon fetching off, VaultCell makes no request to any website for an icon and shows only the built-in and generated ones.

Where fetched icons are stored. They are cached on your device under hashed file names rather than the domains themselves, so the cache does not spell out a list of what you have saved. Clear it under [[FILL: Settings → Privacy → Clear icon cache]]; deleting the app removes it too.

Turning automatic fetching on or off: [[FILL: Settings → Privacy → Show website icons]]. Whatever you choose is respected and is never overridden by us.

9. Security

Your vault is encrypted on your device using [[FILL: e.g. AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your master password using Argon2id]]. Encryption and decryption happen locally; keys derived from your master password never leave your device.

No system is perfectly secure. The practical risk to your vault sits almost entirely at the endpoints you control: the strength of your master password, whether your devices are compromised, and whether your recovery key is stored safely.

2.8 网站图标

VaultCell 会在每个条目旁显示一个小图标，方便你快速定位。图标从何而来至关重要，因此我们在本节完整说明。

  • 内置且离线。常见网站与应用的图标随 VaultCell 一同打包。对这类条目，图标直接取自应用内部，不产生任何网络请求。
  • 本地生成兜底，立即显示。若我们未内置某站点的图标，VaultCell 会先在本地生成一张字母图块，配色由域名派生——因此条目在任何等待期间都不会出现空白。
  • 自动联网获取。在中国大陆以外，默认情况下 VaultCell 随后会访问该条目所保存的域名，下载该站点公开的图标——例如 https://www.paypal.com/favicon.ico。该请求由你的设备直接发往该网站，不经过我们，也不经过我们运营或使用的任何服务器与缓存。我们看不到它，不会记录它，也不掌握你持有哪些条目的任何线索。若你的 App Store 地区为中国大陆，自动获取默认关闭，除非你自己开启，否则 VaultCell 只显示内置图标与本地生成的图标。

这对你意味着什么。我们收不到任何东西。但这些请求并非不可见：你所保存的每个网站都会看到请求来源的 IP 地址；任何能够观察你网络流量的一方——你的网络服务提供商、DNS 解析服务、网络运营者，或你所用 Wi-Fi 或蜂窝网络的管理方——都能看到被请求的域名。HTTPS 加密的是传输内容，而不是"你在与谁通信"。正因如此，我们提供了关闭选项：关闭后，VaultCell 不会为获取图标向任何网站发起请求，只使用内置图标与本地生成的图标。

获取到的图标存在哪里。它们以哈希后的文件名缓存在你的设备上，而非以域名命名，因此缓存本身不会列出你保存了什么。清除路径：[[待填写：设置 → 隐私 → 清除图标缓存]]；删除应用也会一并移除。

开启或关闭自动获取：[[待填写：设置 → 隐私 → 显示网站图标]]。你的选择会被完全尊重，我们不会覆盖它。
