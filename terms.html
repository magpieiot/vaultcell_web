3. The service

VaultCell is a password and secret manager. It stores your passwords, passkeys, cards, and notes in an encrypted vault on your device, and — if you enable it — synchronises that vault across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. There is no user account and no registration; the vault belongs to your device and to your own iCloud account.

We may add, change, or remove features over time. If a change removes functionality you have already paid for, we will refund the unused portion of the affected subscription period on request.

4. Master password and recovery — read this

We cannot recover your vault. VaultCell uses a zero-knowledge architecture. Your master password is never transmitted to us and never stored by us. It exists only in your memory and, while the vault is unlocked, in your device's memory. We hold no copy, no hash we can reverse, and no back door.

This is what makes the app worth using, and it has one unavoidable consequence: if you lose your master password and your recovery key, your data is gone permanently. There is no support process, no identity check, and no override that can get it back. Do not contact us expecting one.

It is your responsibility to:

  • choose a strong master password and remember it;
  • write down the recovery key shown when you create your vault and store it somewhere safe and offline;
  • keep your devices and Apple ID secure, since anyone with access to them and to your master password can open your vault;
  • maintain your own backups if your data matters to you.

We are not responsible for any loss of data arising from a lost, forgotten, or compromised master password, or from a lost recovery key.
