A few things you'll probably ask

Can I take my passkeys to another manager?
Yes. VaultCell supports FIDO CXP/CXF import and export, so passkeys can move between services that support the standard. They are not trapped in VaultCell.
What if I forget my master password?
We cannot reset it. There is no account or server holding your key. When you set up your vault, you get recovery keys to store offline. Keep them somewhere safe, because they are the only way back in.
Can you see my passwords or the sites I save?
No. There is no server-side vault, and we do not run an icon lookup service. Built-in icons are local. If automatic fetching is on, your iPhone contacts the website directly, not us.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. VaultCell works fully offline on your device. There is no sign-up, no cloud account, and no subscription needed for the core features.
Does it sync across devices?
No. VaultCell is deliberately single-device. There is no cloud sync and no cross-device dashboard. That is the tradeoff that keeps the vault local.
