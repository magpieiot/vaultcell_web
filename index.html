Built around the details that matter when you switch

Encryption is the baseline. VaultCell focuses on three things that are usually missed: letting your entries move between managers, keeping your site list out of someone else's logs, and showing what depends on an email address or phone number before you give it up.

Move your entries, not just your passwords

Import and export username/password entries with Apple Credential Exchange on iOS 26+, straight from the iOS Passwords app. Passkeys, one-time codes and cards stay in the app. Switching managers should not mean re-entering every login by hand.

Even the icons keep your secrets

Common icons ship with the app. The rest get a locally generated tile. If you allow automatic fetching, the request goes straight from your iPhone to that website, never through us, and the cache uses hashed filenames rather than your domain list.

Know what breaks before you close an account

VaultCell audits the email addresses and phone numbers used across your vault. Before you close an inbox or give up a number, see exactly which entries depend on it and what you need to update first. The audit runs locally, with no lookup against our servers.

AutoFill without an account

Fill logins in apps and browsers. TOTP codes stay in the app. Everything runs locally on your device.

Common questions

Can I move my entries to another manager?
Yes. VaultCell supports Apple Credential Exchange (iOS 26+) for username/password entries, including from the iOS Passwords app. Passkeys, one-time codes and cards are not transferable in this version. Your saved logins can move with you and are not trapped in VaultCell.
What happens if I close an email account or change my phone number?
VaultCell audits the email addresses and phone numbers referenced in your vault and shows which entries depend on each one. You can update those entries before you lose the inbox or number, instead of finding out when a recovery code lands somewhere you no longer control.
What if I forget my master password?
We cannot reset it — there is no account or server holding your key. Keep your encrypted backup file and its password safe; that is the only way back in.
Can you see my passwords or the sites I save?
No. We operate no server-side vault, and we do not run an icon lookup service. Built-in icons are local. If automatic fetching is on, your iPhone contacts the website directly, not us.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account is needed. Core features work offline; automatic icon fetching is optional and can be turned off. There is no subscription for the core features.
Does it sync across devices?
Yes, optionally. iCloud sync is off by default. Encrypted records go through your own private iCloud database, which we cannot read.
