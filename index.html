A few things you'll probably ask

What if I forget my master password?
Because of our zero-knowledge design, your master password is never uploaded, so we genuinely can't recover it for you. When you set up your vault we generate a set of recovery keys; store them offline, because they're your only backup.
Can you see my passwords?
No. Data is encrypted on your device before it is written to disk, and the decryption key lives only in your device and your master password. Nothing is uploaded, and no server ever holds a copy.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. VaultCell works fully offline on your device. There is no sign-up, no cloud account, and no subscription needed to unlock the core features.
Can I migrate from another password manager?
Absolutely. VaultCell imports export files from major managers and also pulls entries in through an open CSV format, encrypting everything locally as it lands.
